Acme Corporation - New Employee Onboarding Checklist

Purpose: This checklist governs every new hire's first 30 days at Acme, ensuring
consistent, auditable onboarding across all departments.

Before Day 1 (completed by the hiring manager):
Equipment is ordered no later than 5 business days before the employee's start date.
IT provisions a laptop, a company email account, and Slack access 2 business days
before start. Facilities issues a building access badge on the morning of the
employee's first day, not before, for security reasons.

Day 1:
The new employee completes I-9 identity verification and tax withholding forms with
HR before noon. IT enables multi-factor authentication on the employee's corporate
accounts, matching the 7-day MFA enrollment window in the Employee Handbook, though
onboarding aims to complete this on Day 1 itself. The employee attends a 90-minute
company orientation session covering Acme's mission, org structure, and code of
conduct.

Week 1:
The employee completes mandatory security awareness training (45 minutes) and
harassment prevention training (60 minutes), both required within the first 5
business days per Acme's compliance policy. The hiring manager schedules 1:1
meetings with every immediate team member during week 1. The employee is enrolled in
Acme's benefits program, with a 30-day window to make benefit elections before
default coverage is automatically applied.

Day 30:
The hiring manager completes a 30-day check-in survey covering role clarity, team
integration, and any blockers. HR reviews the completed onboarding checklist and
archives it in the employee's personnel file, which is retained for 7 years after
termination per Acme's Data Retention Policy.

Probationary Period:
All new hires (except those in roles covered by a collective bargaining agreement)
are subject to a 90-day probationary period, during which either party may end the
employment relationship with 2 weeks' notice instead of Acme's standard 4-week
notice period for employees past probation.

Remote Employee Additions:
Remote new hires receive equipment via courier rather than in-person handoff, with
delivery confirmed at least 1 business day before their start date. Remote employees
complete the same Day 1 orientation session via video conference and must confirm a
stable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps, matching the Employee Handbook's
remote work requirement, before their equipment ships.
