RoomWise Product FAQ

What is RoomWise?

RoomWise is Acme's property-listing generation tool. It turns a set of room photos and basic property details into a publish-ready real estate listing description in under a minute.

Which file formats can I upload?

RoomWise accepts JPEG and PNG images up to 10 MB each, with a maximum of 25 images per listing.

How much does RoomWise cost?

RoomWise offers three plans: Starter at $29 per month for up to 10 listings, Pro at $79 per month for up to 50 listings, and Agency at $199 per month for unlimited listings with team seats.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the billing page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; there are no cancellation fees for monthly plans.

Does RoomWise support multiple languages?

As of this FAQ's publication, RoomWise generates listing copy in English, Spanish, and French. Additional languages are on the roadmap but not yet available.

Who do I contact for support?

Support requests can be sent to support@acme.example. Pro and Agency plan customers receive a response within 4 business hours; Starter plan customers receive a response within 1 business day.
