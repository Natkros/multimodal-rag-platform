# Acme Corporation — Employee Handbook (FY2026)

## 1. Remote Work Policy

Acme Corporation permits employees to work remotely up to three (3) days per week,
subject to manager approval. Employees who wish to work remotely five days per week
must submit a Flexible Work Arrangement request to HR at least 30 days in advance.

All remote employees must:

- Maintain a stable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps.
- Be reachable via Slack during core hours (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM, local time).
- Attend the weekly all-hands meeting on Mondays at 9:00 AM, either in person or via video.

## 2. Leave and Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, capped at
21 days per calendar year. Unused PTO above 5 days does not roll over into the next
fiscal year.

Parental leave is granted as follows:

| Leave Type            | Duration     | Pay              |
|------------------------|-------------:|------------------|
| Primary caregiver      | 16 weeks     | 100% of base pay |
| Secondary caregiver    | 6 weeks      | 100% of base pay |
| Adoption leave         | 12 weeks     | 100% of base pay |

## 3. Expense Reimbursement

Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable business expenses, including travel,
client meals, and approved software subscriptions. Expense reports must be submitted
within 60 days of the expense date through the Finance portal. Expenses submitted
after 90 days will not be reimbursed except with VP-level approval.

## 4. Cancellation and Refund Policy (Customer-Facing)

Acme's standard customer contracts allow cancellation within 30 days of signing for
a full refund. After 30 days, customers on annual plans may cancel with a pro-rated
refund for unused months, minus a 10% early-termination fee. Monthly plans may be
cancelled at any time with no refund for the current billing cycle.

## 5. Security Requirements

All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on their corporate
accounts within 7 days of hire. Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.
Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@acme.example within
1 hour of discovery.

## 6. Q2 2025 Financial Summary (for RAG evaluation purposes only)

Acme's Q2 2025 revenue was $42.3 million, up 18% year-over-year. The primary growth
drivers were (1) expansion of the Enterprise tier, which grew 34% quarter-over-quarter,
and (2) a reduction in customer churn from 4.1% to 2.8%. Operating expenses were
$31.1 million, resulting in an operating margin of 26.5%.

## 7. Q2 2024 Financial Summary (for RAG evaluation purposes only)

Acme's Q2 2024 revenue was $35.8 million, up 11% year-over-year. Operating expenses
were $27.4 million, resulting in an operating margin of 23.5%. Enterprise tier revenue
represented 22% of total revenue in this period.
