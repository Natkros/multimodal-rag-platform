Acme Corporation Data Retention Policy

Purpose

This policy defines how long Acme retains different categories of data, and the process for deleting data once its retention period expires. It applies to all data Acme collects, processes, or stores, whether on Acme-owned infrastructure or a third-party processor.

Retention Periods by Data Category

Customer account data (name, email, billing address) is retained for the duration of an active subscription plus 90 days after cancellation, to allow for account reactivation. Payment transaction records are retained for 7 years to satisfy tax and financial audit requirements. Application log data containing no personal information is retained for 180 days. Support ticket contents, including any attachments a customer uploads, are retained for 2 years after ticket closure. Employee HR records are retained for 7 years after termination of employment, per Acme's standard employment law compliance guidance.

Deletion Process

Data reaching the end of its retention period is deleted within 30 days via an automated nightly job. Deletion is a hard delete from primary storage; backups containing the deleted data are purged on the next backup rotation cycle, which occurs every 45 days. A customer submitting a formal data deletion request under applicable privacy law (e.g. a GDPR Article 17 request) receives confirmation of deletion within 30 days of the request, faster than the standard rotation-based purge if necessary.

Legal Holds

If Acme's legal team places a legal hold on a data category (for example, during active litigation), that data is exempted from its normal retention schedule and retained until the legal team formally releases the hold. A legal hold overrides the automated deletion job entirely for the affected records.

Third-Party Processors

Any third-party data processor Acme uses must contractually commit to the same retention and deletion timelines as this policy, verified annually via Acme's vendor security review process (see the Vendor Security Policy). Acme's cloud infrastructure provider retains encrypted backups for a maximum of 45 days, matching Acme's own backup rotation cycle.

Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually by Acme's Legal and Security teams jointly, and whenever a new data category is introduced or a relevant law changes. The most recent review was completed in Q1 2026, with no changes to the retention periods above.
